Pastry Dough
All-purpose flour or bread flour (500g / approx. 4 cups)
Lard or shortening (120g / approx. 1/2 cup)
Unsalted butter (120g / approx. 1/2 cup, diced)
Salt (1 tsp)
Cold water (175ml / approx. 3/4 cup)

Filling
Beef skirt or sirloin steak (400g, thinly sliced or diced)
Waxy potatoes (300g, peeled and diced)
Swede / rutabaga / turnip (150g, peeled and diced)
Yellow or brown onion (150g, peeled and diced)
Extra butter (a small amount/knob to dot inside each pasty)
Salt and black pepper (to taste, generous on the pepper)

Glaze
Egg (1 beaten, for egg wash)
