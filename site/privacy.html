Got Dill? — Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

Got Dill? is a shared grocery list for a household. This policy says what it stores, who else can see it, and what leaves your phone. There is no advertising in this app, no analytics or tracking software of any kind, and nothing here is sold or shared for marketing.

What is stored

  • Your account — email address and the display name you choose. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito; your password is held by Cognito and is never visible to us.
  • Your household — its name, its members, and the invite codes used to join it.
  • Your lists — item names, notes, which shop they belong to, who added or crossed off each item, and when.
  • Your shops — the store names, aisles and departments you set up, and which items you have said are in which aisle.
  • Photos you attach to an item.
  • Trip history — a record of shopping trips and what was bought, used to suggest items next time.

This is held in Amazon Web Services in the United States (region us-east-1).

Who can see it

Everyone in your household can see, edit and delete everything in it. That is the purpose of the app — a shared list only works if it is shared. Items you add carry your name so other members can see who added what.

People outside your household cannot see any of it. Access is enforced at the server, not just hidden in the app.

What leaves your phone, and to whom

  • Dictating a list sends that audio recording to OpenAI to be turned into text. See the OpenAI privacy policy.
  • Parsing a list, and working out which aisle something is in, sends the text — and, if you photograph a written list, that photo — to Anthropic. See the Anthropic privacy policy.
  • Saying which aisle you are standing in is recognised entirely on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Nothing is sent to either company unless you use the feature that needs it.

Logs

Server logs record that a request happened, how large it was, and whether it failed. They do not record the contents of your list, your notes, your photos or your recordings.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account from inside the app, in Settings. This removes your sign-in and your membership of the household. Items you added stay with the household, because other members may still need them — unless you were the last member, in which case the household and everything in it is deleted with you.

Children

Got Dill? is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
