# Deleting your Got Dill? account

You can delete your account from inside the app. You do not need to email anyone
or fill in a form.

## How

1. Open Got Dill?
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Scroll to **Danger Zone**
4. Tap **Delete my account**

## What gets deleted

- Your sign-in
- Your user record — your email address, display name, and profile colour

## What stays

Items you added stay with the household. The rest of your household is still
using that list, so their groceries do not disappear when you leave.

## If you are the last member

If you are the only person left in the household, deleting your account also
deletes the household and everything in it — the list, the items, the notes, the
attached photos, and the store and aisle layouts.

## On your phone

"Track record" trip statistics are stored only on your device and are never
uploaded. Delete the app to remove them.
