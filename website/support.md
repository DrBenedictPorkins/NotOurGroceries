## Common questions

**How do I share a list with someone?**
Everyone who joins the same household shares one list.

**Someone else's changes aren't showing up.**
The list syncs when the app has a network connection. If a phone has been offline,
its changes appear once it reconnects.

**The app put an item in the wrong aisle.**
Aisle placement is a guess made by AI, and it gets things wrong. You can correct
it, and you can edit your store's aisle layout by hand.

**Dictation typed the wrong thing.**
Speech-to-text is not perfect. Edit the item after it appears — the app does not
add anything to your list without you confirming it.

**Where do my trip statistics live?**
On your phone only. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete my account**. Full details in
[Account Deletion](account-deletion.md).
